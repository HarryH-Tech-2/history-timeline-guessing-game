Delete your account

History Date Guesser: Quiz by Harry Harrison (Google Play package com.harryhh.historydateguesser)

You can permanently delete your account and everything stored with it at any time. No account is required to play the game, so you can keep playing as a guest afterwards.

Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and go to Profile.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm your identity: enter your password (email accounts) or approve the Google prompt (Google accounts).
  4. Tap Delete my account and confirm. Deletion is immediate.

Or request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email h.harrison1993@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with (or the Google account you used) with the subject "Delete my History Date Guesser account". We verify the sender matches the account and delete it within 30 days, then confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in identity in Firebase Authentication (email address, display name, profile picture link, Google account link, and the internal user ID).
  • All cloud saves: XP, level, coins, hearts, museum collection, campaign progress, daily records and best scores.
  • Your entry on the global leaderboard (display name, XP and level).
  • When deleted from the app, the copy of that progress stored on the device as well.

What is kept

  • Nothing tied to your account is retained after deletion. There is no additional retention period.
  • Purchase and subscription records are held by Google Play under your Google account and are not affected. Cancel an active Premium subscription in the Play Store; deleting the account does not cancel it.
  • Backups of the database that may contain your data are overwritten on their normal rolling schedule and are never restored to the live service without removing deleted accounts again.
See also the privacy policy. Questions: h.harrison1993@gmail.com.
