# Store listing copy — Date Guesser

## Description (Play Games Services · Properties tab, also fine for the Play Store)

Date Guesser is the history quiz where every answer is a year. See a moment
from history — an invention, a battle, a discovery, a masterpiece — and drag
the timeline to when you think it happened. The closer your guess, the bigger
your score.

Four ways to play:
• Daily — eight fresh questions, one shot a day
• Endless — keep guessing and chase a high score
• Survival — three lives, how far can you get?
• Campaign — work through themed worlds and earn stars

Earn XP and coins, level up, unlock achievements, and climb the global
leaderboard against history buffs everywhere. Sign in to keep your progress
safe across devices.

From ancient empires to the space age — how well do you really know when
things happened?

## Short description (Play Store, max 80 characters)

Guess the year of historic events on a timeline. Score, streak, and climb.
