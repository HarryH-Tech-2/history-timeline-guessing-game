## Full description (4000 max)

Guess the year. Master history.

History Quiz: Guess the Year is the history trivia game where every answer is a year. See a famous moment from the past, slide the timeline to when you think it happened, and score up to 1,000 points for landing on the exact year. No multiple choice, no lucky guesses. Just you, the timeline and 2,500 years of world history.

Every one of the 305 questions is illustrated with a hand-crafted miniature scene, and every answer comes with the real story behind the date, so you learn something with every guess.

🗓️ HOW TO PLAY
• Read the moment, look at the scene, and drag the timeline to your year
• Fine-tune with the + and – buttons, one year at a time
• The closer you land, the more you score. Nail the exact year for a perfect 1,000
• Read the story behind the date after every guess

🎮 FOUR WAYS TO PLAY
• Daily Challenge – eight fresh questions, one shot a day. Keep your streak alive and share your score card
• Survival – three lives. How far can you get?
• Campaign – march through five eras, from the Ancient World to the Modern Era, and earn three stars on every stage
• Endless – keep guessing and chase your high score (Premium)

📚 305 QUESTIONS ACROSS 12 CATEGORIES
• Historic Events – from the Great Fire of London to the fall of the Berlin Wall
• Battles – Marathon, Hastings, Waterloo, Stalingrad
• Famous People – when history's icons were born
• Technology – the printing press, the steam engine, the telephone, the World Wide Web
• Exploration & Discovery – Magellan, Shackleton, Everest, the first human in space
• Treaties – Westphalia, the Congress of Vienna, NATO, the Paris Agreement
• Sport – the first Olympics, the four-minute mile, the Rumble in the Jungle
• Money & Trade – the Medici Bank, tulip mania, Bretton Woods, Black Monday
• Arts & Culture – Homer, the Sistine Chapel, Beethoven, the Beatles (Premium)
• Philosophy – Socrates, Kant, Marx, Simone de Beauvoir (Premium)
• Regional – pick Europe, Asia, Africa, the Americas or Oceania and play its defining moments (Premium)
• Space – Laika, Apollo 8, Voyager, the James Webb telescope (Premium)

🏛️ BUILD YOUR MUSEUM
Guess close to the real year to add the artefact to your collection. Fill each wing to earn bronze, silver and gold mastery badges, and complete all 305 exhibits.

🏆 ACHIEVEMENTS, LEVELS AND LEADERBOARDS
• Unlock 28 achievements, from Bullseye to Time Lord
• Earn XP and coins and level up
• Climb the leaderboard today, this week and of all time

🦉 MEET MINERVA
Your owl scholar cheers your perfect years and picks you up after a miss.

✨ MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Warm, clean design with light and dark themes
• Sound effects and vibration, each with its own switch

Perfect for history students, pub quiz regulars, teachers, trivia fans, and anyone who has ever argued about when something happened.

- Title, short and full description, screenshots, icon, video: Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing
- App category and tags: Grow users → Store presence → Store settings (App category → Game → Trivia; Manage tags → up to 5)
