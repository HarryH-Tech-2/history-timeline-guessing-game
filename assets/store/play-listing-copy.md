## Full description (4000 max)

Guess the year. Master history.

History Date Guesser is a history trivia quiz where every answer is a date. See a famous moment from the past — a battle, an invention, a discovery, a masterpiece — and slide the timeline to the year you think it happened. The closer your guess, the more points you score. No multiple choice, no lucky guesses: just you, the timeline, and 5,000 years of world history.

Illustrated with beautiful miniature 3D scenes, it's the history quiz game for anyone who loves trivia, timelines and the story of how we got here.

🗓️ HOW TO PLAY
• Read the event, look at the picture, and drag the timeline to your year
• Pinch to zoom from millennia down to single years, or jump straight to a century
• Score up to 1,000 points per question — nail the exact year for a perfect
• Learn the real story behind every date after each guess

🎮 FIVE WAYS TO PLAY
• Daily Challenge – eight fresh questions, one shot a day, keep your streak alive
• Topic of the Day – a new themed run every day: Ancient Rome, the World Wars, Great Inventions and more
• Endless – keep guessing and chase your high score
• Survival – three lives, how far can you get?
• Campaign – work through worlds and earn three stars on every stage

📚 150 QUESTIONS ACROSS FIVE CATEGORIES
• Historic Events – from the Great Pyramid to the fall of the Berlin Wall
• Battles – Thermopylae, Hastings, Waterloo, D-Day
• Famous People – when history's icons were born
• Technology – paper, the printing press, the telephone, the first nuclear reactor
• Arts & Culture – Homer, the Sistine Chapel, Beethoven, the Beatles

🏛️ BUILD YOUR MUSEUM
Guess close to the real year to add an artefact to your collection. Fill a wing to earn its mastery badge and complete the museum.

🏆 PROGRESS, STREAKS AND LEADERBOARDS

🦉 MEET MINERVA

Perfect for history students, pub quiz regulars, teachers, trivia fans and anyone who has ever argued about when something happened.
