## Full description (4000 max)

Guess the year. Master history.

History Quiz: Guess the Year is the history trivia game with a twist: every answer is a year. See a famous moment from the past, slide the timeline to when you think it happened, and score up to 1,000 points for landing on the exact year. No multiple choice, no lucky guesses. Just you, the timeline and 2,500 years of world history.

Every question is illustrated with a miniature 3D scene, and every answer comes with the real story behind the date, so you learn something with every guess.

🗓️ HOW TO PLAY
• Read the moment, look at the scene, and drag the timeline to your year
• Pinch to zoom from centuries down to single years
• The closer you land, the more you score. Nail the exact year for a perfect 1,000
• Read the story behind every date after each guess

🎮 FIVE WAYS TO PLAY
• Daily Challenge – eight fresh questions, one shot a day. Keep your streak alive and share your score
• Topic of the Day – a new themed run every day: Ancient Rome, the Middle Ages, Great Inventions, the World Wars, Disasters and more
• Campaign – march through six eras from the ancient world to the space age and earn three stars on every stage
• Survival – three lives. How far can you get?
• Endless – keep guessing and chase your high score

## Where things live in Play Console

- Title, short and full description, screenshots, icon, video: Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing
- App category and tags: Grow users → Store presence → Store settings (App category → Game → Trivia; Manage tags → up to 5)
- Developer name: Setup → Developer page (or Developer account → Account details)
- Store listing experiments: Grow users → Store presence → Store listing experiments
