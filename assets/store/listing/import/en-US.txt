APP NAME:
Date Guesser: History Quiz

SHORT DESCRIPTION:
History quiz & trivia game: slide the timeline and guess the year of 538 events

FULL DESCRIPTION:
Guess the year. Master history.

Date Guesser is the history quiz where every answer is a year. See a famous historical event, slide the timeline to when you think it happened, and score up to 1,000 points for landing on the exact year. No multiple choice, no lucky guesses: just you, the timeline and 3,000 years of world history, from 1000 BCE to today.

Every one of the 538 history trivia questions comes with its own illustrated miniature scene, and every answer reveals the story behind the date. It's a history game you learn from: every guess teaches you something new.

HOW TO PLAY
• Read the moment, study the scene and drag the timeline to your year
• Fine-tune one year at a time with the + and − buttons
• The closer you land, the more you score. Nail the exact year for a perfect 1,000
• Read the real story after every guess

WAYS TO PLAY
• Daily – a daily history quiz: eight fresh questions, the same for everyone. Keep your streak alive and share your score card
• Survival – three lives. How far can you get?
• Campaign – journey through five eras, from The Ancient World to The Modern Era. Pick your route, earn three stars on every stage and claim each era's trophy
• Endless – unlimited lives, chase a high score (Premium)
• Challenges – send a friend the same eight questions and see who knows their history, or start a group and compete on weekly XP

538 HISTORY QUESTIONS ACROSS 12 CATEGORIES
• Historic Events, Battles, Famous People, Technology
• Exploration & Discovery, Treaties, Sport, Money & Trade
• Arts & Culture, Philosophy and Space (Premium)
• Regional – pick Europe, Asia, Africa, North America, South America or Oceania and play its defining moments (Premium)

BUILD YOUR MUSEUM
Guess close to the real year and the artefact joins your collection. Fill each wing of the Museum, one exhibit for every question in the game.

MEET MINERVA
