Date Guesser: History Quiz

SHORT DESCRIPTION:
History quiz: know the year it happened. Win quiz night, settle every argument

FULL DESCRIPTION:
Be the person who knows when it happened.

Someone at the table says the Berlin Wall came down in 1991. You know it was 1989. Date Guesser is the history quiz that gets you there: see a famous moment, slide the timeline to the year you think it happened, and find out how close you really are. A few minutes a day and the dates start to stick.

WHAT YOU WALK AWAY WITH
• Settle the argument. Know that the Battle of Hastings was 1066, Constantinople fell in 1453, Waterloo was 1815 and the Moon landing was 1969. To the year, not "sometime around then"
• Be the one your quiz team turns to. 538 events across 12 categories: battles, treaties, famous people, technology, exploration, sport, money and more
• Drop the story, not just the date. Every answer comes with what actually happened, so you have something worth saying at dinner
• See how history fits together. Place 3,000 years on one timeline, from 1000 BCE to today, and you start to notice what was happening at the same time
• Remember it for good. There is no multiple choice to hide behind. You commit to a year, see how far off you were, and that near miss is what makes the date stay

PROVE IT
• Challenge a friend: send them the same eight questions you just played and see who really knows their history
• Start a group with your family, classmates or quiz team and compete on weekly XP
• Climb the leaderboard: Today, Week and All time
• Play the Daily: eight questions, the same for everyone, new every day. Compare scores and keep your streak alive

WATCH YOURSELF GET BETTER
• Score up to 1,000 points a question. Land on the exact year for a perfect score
• Level up through the historian titles and unlock 28 achievements, from Bullseye to Time Lord
• Fill your Museum: guess close to the real year and the artefact joins your collection, one exhibit for every date you now know
• March through the Campaign: five eras, from The Ancient World to The Modern Era, with three stars to earn on every stage and a trophy for each era
• Test your nerve in Survival: three lives, how far can you get?

FIVE MINUTES IS ENOUGH
• The Daily takes a few minutes, with an optional reminder so you don't miss it
• Every question has its own illustrated miniature scene, so the moment is easy to picture and easier to recall
• Fine-tune your guess one year at a time with the + and − buttons
• Free to play, and no account needed to start
• Play in English, Português (Brasil), Español (Latinoamérica) or 日本語
• Light and dark themes, with sound and vibration you can switch off

WHO IT'S FOR
Quiz night regulars who are tired of guessing the century. Students revising dates for an exam. Teachers who want a warm-up the class asks for. History buffs who want to find out what they don't know. And anyone who has ever said "I think it was around then" and wished they were sure.
